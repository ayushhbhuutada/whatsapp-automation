=====================================================================
            WHATSAPP AUTOMATION PRO (LOCAL STANDALONE EDITION)
=====================================================================

This folder is a 100% self-contained, offline-ready copy of your WhatsApp 
Automation engine. You can copy/paste this entire folder anywhere (e.g. 
Documents, D: Drive, USB Drive, or another PC).

HOW TO RUN:
---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Double-click "START_WHATSAPP_BOT.bat"
2. Your browser will automatically open to:
   http://localhost:5000
3. Everything (Multi-Session manager, Anti-Ban AI, Audience upload, 
   Campaign Queue, and Delivery Excel reports) is ready to send!

SAVED PROFILES:
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Your logged-in WhatsApp sessions and database are already bundled in this folder.
- When you run START_WHATSAPP_BOT.bat, your connected accounts will reconnect automatically.

HOW TO STOP:
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- To stop the server, simply close the black Command Prompt window.
